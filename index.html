What Lingi does

Lingi translates dozens of languages entirely on your device using Apple's Translation framework. Download a language pair once, then translate with zero signal and zero data — nothing you type or scan ever leaves your iPhone.

Arabic & Hebrew, done properly

Beyond general offline translation, Lingi goes deeper for Arabic and Hebrew: a built-in offline dictionary, offline camera translation, Arabic diacritics and root/verb reference, Arabizi input, and native on-device pronunciation.

Built the private way

No account, no ads, no analytics SDKs, no tracking. Your translations, saved phrases, and preferences stay on your device — see the Privacy Policy for the full picture.
