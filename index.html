Questions

Does Lingi really work with no internet connection?

Yes, for every downloaded language pair. Translation, camera scanning, Conversation Mode, and the dictionary all run entirely on your device using Apple's Translation framework. The only feature that briefly uses the internet is the optional Currency Converter.

Is my data private?

Everything you translate, save, or scan stays on your device — nothing syncs to iCloud or any server. See the full Privacy Policy for details.

Which languages does Lingi support?

Lingi covers the broad set of languages Apple's on-device Translation framework supports, with extra depth for Arabic and Hebrew — a bundled dictionary, offline camera translation, diacritics, and root/verb reference.

What's the difference between the annual and lifetime plans?

Both unlock the same features. Annual is $14.99/year with a 7-day free trial and auto-renews unless canceled. Lifetime is a one-time $39.99 payment with no renewal, ever.
