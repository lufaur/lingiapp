What Lingi Does

Lingi is an on-device translator for iOS. It helps you:

  • Translate text and speech between dozens of languages, fully offline once a language pair is downloaded
  • Translate text captured with your camera or scanned from documents
  • Hold a two-way spoken conversation across languages ("Conversation Mode")
  • Look up words in a bundled Arabic↔English dictionary, and go deeper into Arabic diacritics, roots, and verb forms
  • Save phrases to a personal phrasebook and reference Gulf Arabic and Hebrew content
  • Convert currencies while traveling (the one feature that uses the internet — see below)

Translation Accuracy

Lingi's translations are generated by Apple's on-device Translation framework and are provided for general communication and travel use. They are not certified or professional translations — don't rely on them for legal, medical, financial, or other situations where a mistranslation could cause real harm. When it matters, use a qualified human translator or interpreter.
