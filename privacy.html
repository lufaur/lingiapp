Privacy Policy

Your privacy is the product, not a footnote

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Our Commitment

Lingi was built around a single idea: translation should work without sending what you say to a server. This policy explains, plainly, what that means in practice.

Data Stored on Your Device

Everything Lingi does — translating text, transcribing speech, recognizing text from your camera, looking up dictionary entries — happens locally, using Apple's on-device Translation, Speech, and Vision frameworks. The following is stored only on your device, using Apple's SwiftData framework:

  • Tracked languages and downloaded language pairs
  • Saved phrases and your personal phrasebook
  • Conversation Mode and Camera Translate history you choose to keep
  • App preferences (appearance, script-display options, currency preference)

Lingi does not sync your data to iCloud or any server. It stays on the device you're using. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Camera, Microphone & Speech Recognition

Lingi asks for these permissions only to power on-device features, and processes everything locally:

  • Camera — to translate text you point it at, and to scan menus and documents
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition — to hear and transcribe what you say during a face-to-face Conversation Mode session, using on-device speech recognition

No photo, video, or audio is uploaded anywhere, saved to a server, or used to train any model.

The One Feature That Uses the Internet

Lingi's optional Currency Converter fetches live exchange rates from a public, no-account exchange-rate API over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. This request sends only the currency codes you're converting between — never your name, location, translations, or any other personal data. Every other feature works fully offline.

What We Don't Collect

Lingi has no account system and no backend server that stores user data. We don't collect:

  • Your name, email, or any personal identifiers
  • Location data
  • The content of your translations, conversations, or camera scans
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA) — Lingi shows no ads

Lingi does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Crash & Performance Diagnostics

Lingi uses Apple's built-in MetricKit framework to receive anonymized, aggregated crash and performance diagnostics directly from iOS — the same on-device mechanism used by most iOS apps. This data is handled by Apple's platform, not a third-party analytics vendor, and contains no personal content from your use of the app.

Subscriptions & Payment

If you purchase Lingi Pro, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple's App Store / StoreKit. Lingi never sees or stores your payment details — Apple manages billing, receipts, and renewal per its own privacy practices.

Children's Privacy

Lingi is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, regardless of age — there's no account system and no data collection to begin with.

Your Rights

Because Lingi doesn't transmit or store your data on any server, there is no remote copy of your information for us to access, export, or delete on your behalf — deleting the app (or its data in Settings) removes everything, immediately, on your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date, and communicated in the app if the change is significant.
